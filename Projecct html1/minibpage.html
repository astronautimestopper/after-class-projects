Where he was born and early life

Julius was born on the 12th or 13th of July in the year 100bc. He grew up in the ancient city of Rome to a prestigious but finacially declinig family. Later he spent his younger years in the bustling city of Subura


His life in power

Julius Caesar was actually never a Roman emperor—he was a Roman general and statesman. His official title was Dictator (ultimately Dictator perpetuus, or dictator for life), which brought an end to the Roman Republic and paved the way for his great-nephew Augustus to become the first true emperor.

His sad ending

Julius Caesar was assassinated on March 15 (the "Ides of March"), 44 BC, by a group of roughly 60 Roman senators led by Marcus Junius Brutus and Gaius Cassius Longinus. The conspirators feared his growing power and perpetual dictatorship
