My Fitness Routine Cards

This page shows a simple football drill and fitness routine for beginners. Each card represents a different exercise or activity to help you stay fit and healthy.

Morning Stretch

Start the day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck.

Time: 10 minutes

Dribbling practice

Dribble in and out of cones or any stones at your disposal to improve ball control and agility.

Time: 20 minutes

Shooting training

Practise different techniques of shooting like a powershot, trivela and rabona to improve your importance on the pitch.

Time: 15 minutes

Recovery Stretches

Spend a few minutes(5-15) stretching your body nd moving it to prevent any injuries and to help your muscles recover.

Time: 15 minutes

Evening Jog

Go for a jog at a slow but steady pace. This helps build your endurance. Once evry few minutes take a break and relax, and whilst relaxing visualize yourself doing well in an actual mathc, this helps boost confidence.

Time: 25 minutes

Rest and Hydrate

Take a break, drink water, and rest your body. This is important for recovery and overall health.

Time: All day

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, coonsistency, and determination.
